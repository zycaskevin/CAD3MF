## Architecture findings closed during implementation

### Hidden graph dependency

Initial use of `trimesh.split`, `trimesh.repair.fix_normals`, and `trimesh.repair.fill_holes` exposed implicit graph-engine dependencies not present in the production worker baseline.

M1-004-001 therefore owns deterministic implementations for:

- face connected components;
- shared-edge adjacency;
- face winding propagation;
- closed-component orientation;
- boundary-cycle discovery;
- safe triangle/quad hole filling.

The core topology baseline no longer requires NetworkX or SciPy graph facilities.

### Scale truth

Bounding-box invariants are computed from vertices actually referenced by faces. Unreferenced vertex-buffer garbage is diagnostic data and cannot change manufacturing scale truth.

### Conservative repair boundary

Boundary loops larger than four vertices, ambiguous boundary graphs, remaining open shells, and remaining non-manifold topology are not aggressively guessed. They remain `needs_robust_repair` for M1-004-002.
